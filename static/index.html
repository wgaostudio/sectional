Sectional

Practice your part at home, with the whole ensemble.

Add a photo or PDF of your music. Sectional reads it, finds your part and plays it with everyone else's, so you can learn it step by step.

  1. 1
    Add your music

    Photos of each page, or a PDF. Reading takes about a minute a page.

  2. 2
    Pick your part

    Tell it what you sing or play, and check it found the right staff.

  3. 3
    Practice in five steps

    From hearing your part and echoing it, to singing or playing it on your own.

The sample is The Turtle Dove, already read, so you can try practicing right away.
